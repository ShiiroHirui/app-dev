# *Harry Potter and the Sorcerer’s Stone*  

**Release Date:** November 16, 2001  
**Directed by:** *Chris Columbus*  
**Based on the novel by:** [J.K. Rowling]

---

## Summary  

When eleven-year-old **Harry Potter** discovers he is a wizard, he’s whisked away to **Hogwarts School of Witchcraft and Wizardry**. Alongside his new friends, **Ron Weasley** and **Hermione Granger**, Harry uncovers the mystery of the **Sorcerer’s Stone** and faces the dark wizard **Voldemort** for the first time.

---

## Main Characters  

- **Harry Potter** — The Boy Who Lived  
- **Hermione Granger** — Intelligent and resourceful witch  
- **Ron Weasley** — Loyal and brave friend  
- **Albus Dumbledore** — Wise headmaster of Hogwarts  
- **Lord Voldemort** — The dark wizard seeking immortality  

---

## Memorable Moments  

1. Hagrid’s famous line: *“Yer a wizard, Harry!”*  
2. The first trip to **Diagon Alley**  
3. The **Sorting Hat** ceremony  
4. Playing **Quidditch** for the first time  
5. The final showdown with **Professor Quirrell**
